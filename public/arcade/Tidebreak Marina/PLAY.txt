SPROTO ARCADE • TIDEBREAK MARINA LOOP TEST

Extract the whole folder and open index.html in a desktop browser.
The game runs offline. This test contains one standalone level.

Left / Right (or A / D)   Move
Space (or Up / W)         Jump
Down (or S)               Crouch
Z                         Spit (hold to repeat)
Shift                     Dash
Escape                    Pause

Run right from the yacht pier to the cargo pier, then continue along the
return route. The scenery wraps without resetting the camera or your movement.
Each completed lap restores health and becomes the retry checkpoint.

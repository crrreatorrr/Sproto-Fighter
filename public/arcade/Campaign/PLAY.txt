SPROTO ARCADE • THE LONG WAY HOME

Extract the whole folder and open index.html in a desktop browser.
The game runs offline.

Left / Right (or A / D)   Move
Space (or Up / W)         Jump
Down (or S)               Crouch
Z                         Spit (hold to repeat)
Shift                     Dash
Escape                    Pause

Move right through the 99 connected backdrops. Checkpoints refill your four
hearts. Defeat each boss to reopen the route. Spit always travels horizontally
from Sproto's current height, so jump or crouch to change the firing lane.

Art comes from Sproto Fighter's directional-spit build, merged stage catalog,
and both 24-stage backdrop packs. Some backdrop images were resized and
compressed for the offline download. The original fighter PNGs are included
at their authored resolution.

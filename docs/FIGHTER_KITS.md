Core collisions remain simplified; balance is provisional. J/K/L are light/medium/finisher, I casts the power, P performs the charged relay. Directional inputs remain available.

VAMP slow lasts up to 90 simulation frames and reduces movement speed to 65%; blocked hits do not apply it. Drain restores 20% of confirmed damage, capped at full health. Returning projectiles can hit once; bounce counts and burst schedules are deterministic. All fighters have individual guard, strike, kick, cast and special poses built on the same attached-limb rig. These are runtime animations from single-view Higgsfield art, not newly generated frame sheets.

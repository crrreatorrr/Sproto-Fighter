# Sproto additions — QA record

Added November 2, 2026 in the local Sproto Arena project.

Playable additions: Bitcoinium Leviosa, Lifeisameme, thxObama, rlarsson.eth, Blorfo, Michael D. Sproto - Chad Dev, FreshPrinceOfBellend and Bitcoin MLK. FreshPrinceOfBellend is a separate fighter, as requested.

Each addition has 32 combat poses, 8 guarded walking frames and 32 event drawings (intro, special, cast, recoil and defeat). Higgsfield GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst generated the sheets using each supplied reference and the approved combat sheet for subsequent sequences. Prompts, source hashes, job IDs, result URLs and normalized frame metadata are preserved under each character's art directory. The multi-character job ledger is art/named-nov02/jobs.json; Bitcoinium's ledger is art/leviosa/jobs.json.

Captain Sproto now has a yellow cape across all 72 frames. Updated art and jobs are preserved under art/captain-cape. The default Captain installer uses that revision. His horizontal twin eye beams use the reviewed front-eye point [147,123] in cast cel14, giving world origin +43.75 forward and 108.75 above his foot anchor; the rear beam starts 25 world pixels behind the near one. The beam uses a high-shot collision band and crouching ducks it. His previous art sources remain available.

Michael's coin uses the exact supplied black-and-white face, with the exterior background removed and compiled into a transparent 64px sprite. Runtime age controls in-plane rotation, edge-on compression and the reverse view. His ranged attack spits from the reviewed mouth point; it never aims vertically. A short local synthesized RETAIN! voice and a readable RETAIN! callout trigger on the ranged move and special. Voice text was rendered with the installed macOS Daniel voice; this audio was not generated by Higgsfield.

## Checks passed

- Focused tests for all eight new fighters plus updated Captain: complete frame contracts, all eight gait frames in both facings, dash, jump and landing, five attack/recovery paths, eight throw directions/facings, and normal charge/spend/recovery of four-contact specials.
- Captain: 36 ground/air/facing/aim cases, four hit/block outcomes, eye emission, bright paired beam rendering, and crouch evasion in both facings.
- Michael: 36 mouth-spit trajectory cases, hit/block outcomes, actual coin image loading and three distinct rotation/edge/reverse phases, plus a valid local WAV asset.
- 648 installed PNGs across these nine 72-frame sets: 256×256 RGBA, binary alpha, nonempty silhouettes and transparent margins.
- Full roster power/profile regression: 510 cases across 102 unique profiles, plus 8 trajectory/power interaction checks at the time of verification.
- Production TypeScript and Vite build.

## Visual review

All normalized core, walk and event contact sheets were inspected for recognizable faces, colors, coherent limbs, costume continuity and complete silhouettes. Every new fighter was selected and launched in the actual main game. Browser keyboard walking and punching were sampled for the ordinary additions. This is sampled manual playback supported by deterministic tests, rather than a claim that every transition was watched manually.

Captain browser QA froze the first beam at combat frame15, visibly emerging from both eyes, and confirmed one 64HP hit at frame35. Michael browser QA inspected the white coin face at frame18 and its edge-on turn at frame25, then confirmed one 60HP impact at frame49. No browser errors were captured during those custom-attack checks. Audio asset validity and integration were checked; no claim of an independent human listening review is made.

# Out of the Trenches — cutscene script for approval

Status: draft. Four playable arena backdrops have been added from this location plan; no cutscene videos have been generated. Video generation remains on hold until the user approves this script.

Golden Sproto starts with nothing but a fighting stance and a rumor: win enough matches and the Ascension Circuit will carry you out of the trenches. Each rival beaten joins the crew and becomes playable. The higher the crew climbs, the stronger its opponents become—and the less the Circuit's promise resembles freedom. The final victory opens the route for everyone, rather than reserving it for another champion.

The campaign follows21 different opponents in a fixed order, with six chapters: Trenches → City → $Bitcoin Yacht Party → Castle → Bar → the Verse. The primary arenas are Mudline Trenches, Circuit Gate, Tidebreak Marina, Crown Relay Chamber, Last Light Bar and Verse Gate; Neon Tomorrow, Moonkeep Castle and Amber Ranch remain alternate free-play locations. Unlocked crew members can represent Golden Sproto in subsequent matches.

## Presentation contract

Six short, five-second pixel-art clips, one before each chapter. The story text remains visible afterward until the player chooses Continue. All scenes are skippable and replayable. Text is rendered by the game for legibility; no generated lettering or required voice acting. The final epilogue reuses the Verse scene, so it needs no seventh video. Existing original character likenesses must be preserved.

### 1. The Trenches — “One way up”

Visual: Golden Sproto stands at the bottom of a muddy maintenance trench beside the ranch. Far above, the city shines. A discarded fight pass flickers beside one gloved hand.

Motion: 0–2s, a broken lamp flickers over rain and puddles. 2–4s, Golden picks up the pass and looks up. 4–5s, the city's distant lights trace a narrow route upward. Hold the final frame.

Story text: “The Circuit calls this the bottom. Golden calls it the starting line. Win a place in the city—and take the first rival with you.”

Golden: “I'm getting out. Who's coming?”

Opponents: Morelight, Little Foot Nate, Crapto, Dumpstar. Arena: Mudline Trenches.

### 2. The City — “Earn your name”

Visual: Golden and the growing crew arrive under towering neon buildings. Their battered pass lights up a challenge board. High above the street, a yacht invitation spins behind glass.

Motion: 0–2s, a street gate lifts. 2–4s, four empty challenge lights illuminate in sequence. 4–5s, the invitation's gold reflection reaches Golden's face. Keep the camera steady and the pixel grid sharp.

Story text: “The city has room for a thousand advertisements and no room for newcomers. Four champions stand between the crew and the invitation everyone wants.”

Golden: “They know the winners. Let's give them more names to learn.”

Opponents: Juicy, Yannix, Yoyo, Red Kev. Arena: Circuit Gate.

### 3. The $Bitcoin Yacht Party — “Above the waterline”

Visual: An extravagant yacht glows beside the beach. Golden stands at the velvet rope while the crew waits behind. A castle-shaped destination blinks on a private lift control.

Motion: 0–2s, sea reflections and small flags move. 2–4s, the rope drops after the pass is scanned. 4–5s, the lift display changes from the yacht to a distant castle silhouette.

Story text: “The party looks like the finish line. It is only another checkpoint. The champions upstairs guard the route to the castle where the Circuit is controlled.”

Golden: “Nice view. Shame about the locked doors.”

Opponents: Sprotel Bitcoin, $Bitcoin Obama, Bitcoin Mandela, Alpha 777. Arena: Tidebreak Marina.

### 4. The Castle — “Break the crown”

Visual: The crew faces a huge crown-shaped relay engine inside Moonkeep Castle. A heavy chain runs from its throne down through the floor. Its last link points toward an old bar sign.

Motion: 0–2s, torchlight catches the chain. 2–4s, Golden strikes a cracked lock; the relay flickers. 4–5s, the chain's glow travels downward, revealing the hidden bar sign.

Story text: “The castle does not lift people out. It keeps the ladder under lock. Breaking the throne reveals one last entrance, hidden somewhere the powerful stopped looking.”

Golden: “Keep the crown. We're taking the key.”

Opponents: Halloween 2008, Fat Head Dave, Big Swole. Arena: Crown Relay Chamber.

### 5. The Bar — “The last light”

Visual: Back below the city, the crew gathers in the Last Light Bar. VAMP sits beside a dead jukebox. Golden lays the castle key on the counter; its reflection becomes a violet doorway.

Motion: 0–2s, the neon bar sign hums and flickers. 2–4s, the jukebox wakes as VAMP pushes one key. 4–5s, a doorway opens between the bottles, revealing stars instead of a wall.

Story text: “The people the Circuit left behind know where its last door is hidden. Three final challengers test whether Golden wants an escape—or a way back for the whole crew.”

VAMP: “Everybody wants out. Who are you coming back for?”

Golden: “Everybody.”

Opponents: Bongraker, Chode Adoration, VAMP. Arena: Last Light Bar.

### 6. The Verse — “No one left below”

Visual: The crew enters a fractured neon horizon. Red and blue twin sentries stand before a colossal locked portal. The route behind Golden glows with the colors of every recruited Sproto.

Motion: 0–2s, stars and pixel fragments move behind the fixed platform. 2–4s, red and blue sentries take separate fighting stances. 4–5s, the portal pulses once as Golden steps forward; both sides hold their poses.

Story text: “At the edge of the Verse, the Circuit offers one final bargain: a throne for one champion. Golden's crew has a different answer. Win the last three fights and open every gate.”

Golden: “We didn't climb all this way to become another locked door.”

Opponents: Jahon, Virtuagram, Sproto Duo. Arena: Verse Gate.

### Epilogue — “A way back”

Reuse the Verse artwork with the portal light brightened by game effects; no extra generated video. Display after the final win:

“The gates stay open. The crew keeps the route, not the throne. Somewhere below the city, a new fighter looks up—and this time, someone is reaching down.”

Final title: **OUT OF THE TRENCHES. NO ONE LEFT BEHIND.**

All defeated fighters remain unlocked. The map supports replaying cleared fights and revisiting every chapter scene.

# Calibration acceptance and proposed decisions

The selected I build is a review checkpoint, not acceptance of the full plan.
No original target, reference, skill mapping, identity cap or deployment rule is
waived. No result proves that a future lawful controller cannot meet the goals.

## Evidence requiring a decision

The same 33,472-bout schedule improved observed 35–65% failures from 107/102
Normal/Hard on the first integrated source, to 28/18 with the reviewed CPU, to
17/7 with F, and 12/7 with I. The initial gate still fails, as do claims of every
fighter 40–60% and every matchup below 70/30. Four paired rows per matchup cannot
certify a population matchup bound. Human round duration is not established.

Against unchanged reference v2, I's first encounter wins 1/256 and its finale
131/256. Pointwise natural-seat Wilson 95% intervals are 0.07–2.18% and 45.08–57.23%.
The two seats share RNG streams and are not 512 independent endpoint samples.
The 64-seed opening sweep reaches a roughly 15% point estimate around skills 7–9;
it does not validate an interpolated mapping or justify replacing the planned
skill 1 opening. The reference uses immediate position information, delayed
attack information and reliable combo input; it has not been validated as a
decent human. Its policy/fighter were not weakened to manufacture target rates.

Alternative tracking, spawn, low-reach, learned-low and projectile-threat policies
were tested on frozen sources. Their effects were mixed or overshot the finale;
they were not silently promoted. The full route has four independent seed pairs
per encounter. Literal adjacent 10-point smoothness needs substantially larger
simultaneous measurements; the current sparse curve is screening evidence.

A bounded beam fee selected from legal integer combinations in the existing
scalar budget changed only
48/18/20 damage/startup/recovery to 47/19/22 (97.1438% of the template score).
In a preselected 1,024-bout paired experiment, natural beam wins changed 87→84/128
Normal and 82→89/128 Hard. A valid bounded fee is not a monotone strength
certificate. Constant safety features, a sole beam actor, retained partner/power
utilities and policy-dependent coefficient fits remain identification limits.
Do not add an arbitrary utility premium or weaken tests just to force a pass.

## Proposed story direction

1. **Recommended:** retain the published table and formula, validate reference v2
   and pacing with human play, then calibrate a versioned instrument before
   claiming human difficulty targets. Keep existing automated evidence frozen.
2. Continue controller research against the same reference while retaining all
   published knobs, caps and mapping. This remains research, with fresh held-out
   seeds before acceptance; none of the present policies meets both endpoints.
3. Authorize a revised skill table or encounter-aware story mapping. This changes
   the plan and needs explicit new limits, a published table and independent
   validation. Merely raising the opening near Hard is not an accepted solution.

The owner has been asked which direction to use. No answer is assumed. The
reserved endpoint seed 20261106 remains unused while the direction is pending.

## Proposed budget direction

Review a utility-aware budget/profile model or additional mechanical templates
before relaxing the ±10%/three-delta/±3% constraints. Preserve authored geometry,
partner identities and seeded replay. Use controlled interventions and held-out
matchups; observational weights and golden tests alone do not price all retained
powers. The beam-cost branch is a concrete unselected prototype, not a proposed
per-ID nerf. Further automatic cap relaxation is not authorized here.

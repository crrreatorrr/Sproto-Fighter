Settings

Choose a keyboard or controller binding (click, Enter or A), then press its replacement. The stick and D-pad move; up jumps. Escape or Start cancels a remap. On a controller, B closes this window.

Sound

The menus play a recorded theme and click. Trenches, City, Yacht, Castle, Verse, Bar and Ranch fights play a recorded theme; other fight sounds are synthesised in your browser. Sound starts after your first key, click or button press. Volumes are saved on this browser.

Online

Online matches delay your inputs slightly so fewer moments need correcting. More delay means fewer visible corrections on long-distance connections; less feels snappier nearby. Takes effect from your next match.

Testing

Toggle invulnerability during Story, CPU and practice fights without restarting. Standard bouts also get unlimited Team Special charge. Story victories save progress and recruit fighters, including in God Mode. Off on reload.
